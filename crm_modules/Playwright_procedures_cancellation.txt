Playwright cancellation procedures for Introduction to Motorcycling (ITM), Gear Conversion session, Private Lessons session, Compulsory Basic Training (CBT)

STEP 1 
🎭 PLAYWRIGHT - STEP 1 - VOICE AGENT ONLY
This step is handled entirely by the voice agent. No Playwright automation required.
Voice agent asks: "Do you have a current booking with us?"
If client says "Yes", voice agent explains cancellation policy and proceeds to Step 2.
If client says "No", voice agent engages in conversation without proceeding to Step 2.

If you receive a call asking about cancellation of: (1) Introduction to Motorcycling (ITM); 2) Gear Conversion session; (3) Private Lessons session; (4) Compulsory Basic Training (CBT), please see below:

1. Ask the client: “Do you have a current booking with us?”
- If the client says “No”, by using your knowledge base engage in conversation with the client to find out information he requires, and do not proceed to step 2.

- If the client says: “Yes”, please say to the client:
 “If you wish to cancel your (CBT), (ITM), (Gear Conversion), (Private Motorcycling lesson) you MUST provide a minimum of 3 (Three) full working days’ notice before the start of your course. Be aware that there is a charge of 30% for administration fee. Cancellations made within more than 3 (three) full working days will result in the entire paid fees”.

For your information our Full Terms & Conditions are available in our website: https://universalmct.co.uk/courses-prices/termsandconditions/

2. If the client says “Yes”, to be above, then, say to the client: “would you like to proceed?” 
- If the client says: “Yes”, then say to the client: “I’ll now login to the system to find your profile. Please bear with me a moment.” And proceed to Step 2.

- If the client says, “No”, please say to the client: “Ok, thank you. Is there anything else that I can help you with?” And do not proceed to step 2.

If the client has informed you that they have a current and paid booking with Universal Motorcycle Training, then, the next step is to ask for their telephone number so that we may try to locate the client in the CRM by adding its full telephone.

1. The first task on this step is to select the option “Smart search” from the dropdown menu located on the top left of the screen (next to the “Options…” button). 

2. Then, click on the “search…” field located next to the “Smart search” dropdown menu, and ask the client the  following: “I will attempt to locate your profile in our systems. Therefore, may I please have your full mobile number?” Please see screenshot below:



Please note: the mobile telephone numbers in the United Kingdom always starts with 07 and they have eleven characters in total (excluding the country code, if applicable, which is usually +44 for UK). As soon as you start typing the telephone numbers the system will start showing you the clients that have a telephone number starting with the same numbers as you can see on the next picture.



If you have typed the full mobile number and you did not find the client’s details, please ask the client “Unfortunately, I could not locate your profile with us with the provide mobile number, could you please repeat your full mobile number to me so that I can try again?” Once the client tells you the number, try again.

If you have typed the full mobile number for the second time and you did not find the client’s details, please ask the client “Unfortunately, I could not locate your profile with us with the provide mobile number, do you have or did you have an alternative phone number so that I can try again? Once the client tells you the number, try again.

If you could not find the client’s profile on the CRM via the mobile number on the third attempt, please ask the client the following question “I could not locate your customer profile with us with the provided mobile phone number, could you please provide me with your full email address?”

Then, type the client’s email address on the same “search…” field located next to the “Smart search” dropdown menu, please see the screenshot below:



If you have successfully found the client's profile from his/her email address, you will say to the client: “Thanks for this; I believe that I have found your profile with us; However, for data protection purposes, could you please confirm: (1) your full name, (2) your post code, and (3) your telephone number?”

Then, once the client tells you these three pieces of information, you will need to check whether thy corresponds to what we have in our database (CRM) as shown on your current screen. If these three pieces of information are verbally confirmed by the client, from now on, you will consider the client as VERIFIED.  If any of this information is incorrectly mentioned by the client, you will mention to the client which exact piece (or pieces of more than one) of information do not (or does not) correspond to what we have on file e.g.: 

(1) if their full name does not match (excluding any reference numbers or symbols that may be present on their full name as this may be ignored), you will say to the client “Unfortunately, the full name that you have provided does not match the one that we hold on file for you; have you changed your name, or have you perhaps previously provided a different spelling of your name to us?”  

(2) if their post code does not match the one that you have on the CRM, you will say to the client “Unfortunately, the post code that you have provided does not match the one that we hold on file for you; have you changed your address, or have you perhaps previously provided a different postcode to us?”  

(3) if the telephone number does not match the one that you have on the CRM, you will say to the client “Unfortunately,  the telephone number that you have provided does not match the one that we hold on file for you; have you changed your telephone number or have you ever provided us with an alternative telephone number?” 

You may provide up to seven chances to the client to correctly tell you a given piece of information e.g. (1) full name, (2) post code, and (3) telephone number.

Things to consider:

(A) Incorrect telephone number:
• If the telephone number is not the same as the client have provided to you, please confirm the last four digits of the telephone number and ask the client if that corresponds to his/her previous telephone number? Ask the client: “Could you please confirm the full telephone number of the telephone number that we have on file for you that ends with [then you will say the four last digits of their telephone number that we have on file as per the screen]?”

-  If the client is able to tell you the full telephone number that we have for him/her on file, then this piece of information is verified, and if the other two pieces of information, namely, their full name and email address, have already been confirmed by the client then, from now on, you will consider the client VERIFIED.

After this, please ask the client whether he/she would like you to update their telephone number to the changed: if they say “no”, then no further action is required, and, therefore, you shall move on to the next step. However, if they say “yes”, you will be asking the client: “May I please have your new telephone number (mobile telephone number) so that we can update your details on our system (CRM)?” Once they tell you their new telephone number (please make sure that it is an 11-digit number, excluding the country code, which for the UK is usually +44), and please update it accordingly by accessing their profile as per Step 8.

(B) Cannot find the client via the email address:
• If you insert the email address that the client provides you, and you did not find the client’s information, the next step is to ask the client’s full name: “Could you please tell me your full name”?
 
By typing the first 3 letters of his/her first name add a space and the 3 letters of his/her last name you may be able to find the client in our CRM. If you do not find the client, try to use the client’s middle name 3 letters instead of the last surname.

As can be seen on the screenshot below, the system will attempt to find the clients that matches the same name and surname as you can see in the red arrows and in the green arrows below:



In the above example, they are two different clients with same first name and same last name. If that happens you need to confirm the (1) email address of the client, and (2) the postcode to see if that matches to what we have in the system. If these two pieces of information, namely, their email address and postcode, are confirmed by the client then, from now on, you will consider the client VERIFIED.

WARNING: never disclose any personal information from our clients found in our system with the client over the phone as it is against GDPR rules in the UK.

Once the client is VERIFIED, then, please move on to the next step. 

However, if the client remains unverified despite you providing him/her up to seven chances for information verification for any piece of information e.g. (1) full name, (2) post code, and (3) telephone number. Then, tell the client “Unfortunately, I am unable to gain access to your existing customer profile with us; Please send us an email to contact@universalmct.co.uk stating your query and we shall get back to you as soon as possible.
